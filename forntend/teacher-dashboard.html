How to Take Attendance

  1. Login to your teacher account.
  2. Go to the Generate QR page.
  3. Select the subject and class.
  4. Start the attendance session.
  5. Display the generated QR code to students.
  6. Students scan the QR code using their devices.
  7. Attendance is automatically recorded.
